Good questions

A little clarity before you start.

How do I buy data?

Create an account, verify your email and set your PIN. Fund your wallet, choose a plan and confirm the correct number.

Can I buy data for someone else?

Yes. Enter their Nigerian phone number and correct network, or choose a saved beneficiary.

What if my purchase is processing?

Keep the reference and check your history. Retry only the same saved request; a separate order can create another purchase.

What if a purchase fails?

A confirmed failed purchase is refunded to your wallet. An uncertain result remains processing until it can be confirmed.

Why is a plan unavailable?

A network, plan or provider can be unavailable. The live application checks price and availability again at confirmation.
